01 / PROTECT

Warranty powder coating

Powder coating is an advanced finishing process where dry powder is electrostatically applied to metal surfaces (especially aluminum and other substrates) and cured under heat to form a strong, uniform, and protective layer.

  • Proper surface pretreatment
  • High-grade powders
  • Controlled coating thickness
  • Uniform curing process
  • Strong adhesion and lasting finish
02 / TRANSFORM

Wood lamination for aluminum

We provide advanced wood-finish coating on aluminium profiles.

  • Realistic wood grain texture
  • Termite-proof and weather-resistant
  • Low maintenance
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor use
  • For doors, windows, facades, and interiors
WARRANTY-BASED FINISHING

Confidence in
the coating.

Warranty powder coating ensures that the coated surface maintains color stability, adhesion strength, corrosion resistance, and surface durability for a specified number of years under defined conditions.

Long life (5–15+ years depending on specification). Resistance to UV rays, moisture, and chemicals. Consistent color and texture. Strong adhesion – no peeling or cracking. Eco-friendly and sustainable process.
